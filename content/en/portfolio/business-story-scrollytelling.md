description: >-
  An interactive series retelling six businesses — refining, aromatics, olefins, polymer, base oil and
  lubricants — as scroll-driven animated stories. 16 pages in Korean and English, built and shipped in
  two days.
problem: >-
  Introducing the company's businesses was always vague. Information sat scattered across the website,
  and the decks each division had were thin. Everything converges at the Yeosu complex, yet that
  connection was hard to show. Could the whole business be seen at once — grounded in accurate data,
  and ideally linked into a story? We needed material people would actually read.
approach:
  - Designed one signature scene per business — fractions splitting in the distillation column, a branch attaching to the benzene ring, a chain snapping to leave a double bond — each tied to the scroll
  - 'Cross-checked the official site, the 2025 sustainability report, gspolymer and kixxoil down to the product-level pages. Where figures conflicted (aromatics: 2.8M vs 3M tons), the sources were weighed and reconciled into one'
  - Put an etymology corner in every chapter — aromatics (fragrance), olefins (the oil-making gas), polymer (many parts) — giving unfamiliar names a story
  - Used the 40 photographs from the Media Hub black-and-white exhibition as they were, with an index and a value-chain journey page on top, threading the chapters through a six-color system
  - Built an English edition so the divisions could use it as a digital leaflet
  - Ran the whole process in Claude Code — research, build, browser verification, Vercel deployment
outcome:
  - 16 pages shipped in Korean and English — one link that walks through every business
  - Found an outdated 2.8M-ton aromatics figure on the site intro, and filed it for correction
  - Ready to serve as shared material for recruiting sessions, press briefings and overseas partners
