tagline: Furniture destined for disposal became one-of-a-kind works of art.
description: >-
  Korea's first large-scale furniture upcycling hackathon. Using furniture
  damaged in transit and slated for disposal, 30+ participants — from elementary
  school students to carpenters — spent two days at a warehouse on Amtae Island,
  Shinan County, creating works that were then exhibited at Donuimun Museum
  Village in Seoul and donated to the community.
problem: >-
  A company Slack message revealed that 1,000+ damaged furniture items would be
  bulk-discarded from the warehouse. "It's such a waste to throw away perfectly
  usable furniture" sparked the proposal. Not a donation-check CSR, but a
  project rooted in the company's own business — furniture — with a real story
  to tell.
approach:
  - >-
    Visited the warehouse to assess discarded furniture firsthand; found the
    DECAL Cooperative with past upcycling experience to kick off the project
  - >-
    Chose a former rice warehouse repurposed as a brewery — a space whose own
    story aligned with the upcycling ethos
  - >-
    30+ participants — elementary school students, carpenters, media artists,
    and street art painters — created approximately 30 works over two days
  - >-
    Seoul's Donuimun Museum Village exhibition "Ohouse, Life of Tomorrow"
    (December 27, 2022 – March 19, 2023) — hackathon works displayed alongside
    pieces by artists Naul, Hoehwa Yuhi, and Kim Kyung-su
  - >-
    After the exhibition, all works donated in full to a repurposed school,
    small libraries, and children's welfare facilities in Shinan County
outcome:
  - >-
    Approximately 30 upcycled works created — bicycle racks, infinity mirror
    tables, tidal flat drawer units, all one-of-a-kind pieces
  - >-
    After 3 months of free exhibition, all works donated to Shinan; the value
    of resource circulation proven through story
  - >-
    Introduced in media as Korea's first large-scale furniture upcycling
    hackathon; established as the company's inaugural CSR case
